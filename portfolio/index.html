Reguleran — manage songs, setlists & live sessions

A music rehearsal and gig-management app for bands and worship teams. Organize your song library, build setlists, track practice sessions, and send client-facing booking proposals — from one place.

Features

What Reguleran does.

Song library

Store lyrics, chord charts, and song sections. Transpose on the fly and attach rehearsal audio.

Setlists

Sequence songs for each event with per-song transpose and notes.

Session tracking

Log rehearsal sessions per setlist, mark songs ready, and follow progress over time.

Schedule & bookings

Keep gigs and services on a calendar, and generate PDF booking proposals for clients.

Band profile

Public-facing profile with photos, genres, and social links.

Auth & multi-device

Secure sign-in with Clerk on the web app and an Expo React Native companion app.
